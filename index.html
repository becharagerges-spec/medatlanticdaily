The cabinet met at the Grand Serail on 15 October with five ministers absent, including Finance and Energy. Industry Minister Joe Issa El Khoury said the Lebanese Forces ministers walked out because electricity had not received a decision matching its urgency, and that Energy Minister Joe Saddi has not resigned.
