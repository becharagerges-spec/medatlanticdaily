Receiving French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot and Armed Forces Minister Alice Rufo at Baabda on 17 October, President Joseph Aoun said a Lebanese military delegation will attend the trilateral meeting at CENTCOM in Tampa on 20 October to press for an end to Israeli attacks.
